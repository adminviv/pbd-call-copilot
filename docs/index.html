PBD Call Copilot

A private panel beside your Zoom call. It listens, then suggests what to ask next, in plain words, with the paper behind it. It never joins the call.

Set up on Windows (one time, about 5 minutes)

  1. Open the downloaded PBD-Call-Copilot-Setup.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. This happens once because the app is new. No administrator password is needed.
  2. Click Next and Install. The app opens when it finishes.
  3. Paste the PBD key Genevieve sends you. It is saved only on this computer.
  4. Next time, open it from the Start menu: type "PBD Call Copilot".

Set up on Mac (one time, about 5 minutes)

  1. Double-click the downloaded zip, then drag PBD Call Copilot into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious software: click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, and confirm. This happens once because the app is new.
  3. Paste the PBD key Genevieve sends you. It is saved only on this computer.
  4. Allow the microphone when asked. Then, in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on PBD Call Copilot and choose Quit & Reopen. This lets it hear the other people on the call; nothing is recorded or saved.
  5. Right-click its icon in the Dock and choose Options → Keep in Dock.

During a call

Starts by itself

When a Zoom meeting opens, the panel switches on. When the meeting ends, it stops.

Off when you want

Click the toggle, or press Ctrl+Alt+P (Mac: Control+Option+P), to pause or resume.

Two cards at a time

What to ask next, what it means for PBD, the terms explained, and a link to the paper.

Screen sharing

On Windows the panel is hidden from screen shares. On a Mac, share a single window rather than your whole screen.

Private

Audio is never saved or sent. Speech is turned into text on your computer. Only the text goes to Claude.
