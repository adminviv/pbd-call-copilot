Privacy policy

PBD Call Copilot · Effective October 27, 2026

PBD Call Copilot is a desktop app made by PBD Project, a nonprofit research accelerator for peroxisome science (pbdproject.org). During a video call, it listens on your computer and shows plain-language explanations and suggested questions. This policy explains what it accesses, where that information goes, and the choices you have.

What the app accesses

  • Your Google Calendar, read-only. When you sign in with Google, the app can read the calendars your account can see: event titles, times, attendees' names and email addresses, descriptions and video-call links. It also reads your email address, only to show which account is signed in. It cannot change your calendar and never accesses Gmail or any other Google data.
  • Call audio, only while you switch it on. When you turn the Copilot on during a confirmed meeting, it hears your microphone and the sound your computer plays. Audio is turned into text on your computer. Audio is never saved and never sent anywhere.

How it uses that information

  • Calendar events are used to list your meetings in the app, to allow the Copilot only during confirmed meetings, and to give the AI the meeting's title, attendee names and agenda so its explanations fit the meeting.
  • Text of the conversation (the latest stretch plus some earlier context) and the meeting details above are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the explanations and suggested questions.
  • Short search terms (a few science words) are sent to Europe PMC to find research papers, and paper IDs are sent to the US National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) to confirm each paper exists.

What is stored, and where

  • On your computer only: your Google sign-in token, the app's access key, and a text transcript of each call in Documents\PBD Call Copilot\transcripts (Documents/PBD Call Copilot/transcripts on a Mac). You can delete these files at any time.
  • PBD Project does not run a server for this app and does not receive copies of your calendar, audio or transcripts.
  • Text sent to Anthropic is handled under Anthropic's commercial API terms.

Google user data

PBD Call Copilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI models, and not shared with anyone except as described on this page to provide the app's features.

Your choices

  • The Copilot never listens unless you switch it on, and you can switch it off at any time.
  • Sign out in the app to remove its access to your calendar, or remove it at any time from your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps with account access.
  • Delete saved transcripts from the folder named above.

Contact

Questions or requests: admin@pbdproject.org. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
